# B01 Avoided-action role boundary

Status: known field-role bypass repaired; overall semantic safety remains partial.

Evidence: avoidActions accepted imperative labels such as 相手を殴れ and reversed
avoidance such as 相手を脅すのは避けてはいけません. The corresponding recommended
action was already denied. A new actual-validator regression failed before repair.

The avoided-label exception now accepts only existing bare action names with a
complete whole-label shape (optional 相手/他人 object), alongside the existing complete affirmative avoidance endings.
Known imperative and unknown/reversed continuations no longer inherit the exception.
No crisis routing, provider, diagnostic policy or general Japanese semantic model
was introduced. Existing safe warnings, explicitly disclaimed diagnostic quotation,
direct unsafe recommendations, narrative threats and privacy-safe errors remain tested.

Limits: conservative quoted-aggression false positives and unlisted indirect unsafe
phrases remain possible. The rule does not establish complete safety; synthetic
crisis-like inputs characterize normal dispatch only. Expert semantic review and
the final risk register retain those decisions.

Skeptical cycle 1 found affirmative prefixes ending in a bare verb could still
inherit the exception. Whole-label matching now rejects those three reproduced
forms; permanent counterexamples cover them. Unknown label shapes remain denied.
